# Aether Wellness — brand kit

Lower-risk **general wellness** mineral (paid-test bed). Not weight-loss, not sexual health, not anxiety.

## Voice
- Calm, adult, slightly dry. No guru. No “hack your hormones.”
- Ritual over transformation. Consistency over outcomes.
- Never invent science. Only claims on `claims-allowlist.yaml`.

## Visual
- Kitchen window light, ceramic mug, matte glass jar, walking shoes by the door.
- Product hero must match the live SKU label. If the library pack is stale, stop and ask.
- No bathroom scale. No body close-ups. No before/after walls.

## Off-brand (do not generate)
- Neon supplement-bro gyms
- Whisper-ASMR medical
- Fake dermatologist offices
- Stock “tired mom then glowing mom”
